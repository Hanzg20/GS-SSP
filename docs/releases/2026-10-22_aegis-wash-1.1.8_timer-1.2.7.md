# Aegis Wash 1.1.8 / Aegis Timer 1.2.7 -- scanner fix, voice volume

## Scanner would not open after the kiosk screen was recreated (the real cause)
`WizarPosScannerProvider` kept the Activity it was created from, and the cloudpos SDK binds the
scan service with that context on every `open()`. Once that Activity was destroyed (OPC covering
the kiosk, a HOME relaunch -- the provider outlives it), the system refused the binding
("W/ActivityManager: Binding with unknown activity") and `open()` returned false in 1-30 ms:
253 on 2026-10-07 / 10-21, 255 on 10-22 from 22:58 (task 3089 -> 3090) on. Scans failed until
the app process restarted. Now it uses the application context. 1.1.7's retry/diagnostics stay
(the diagnostics showed "camera permission ok; cam0=free" -- the camera itself was fine).

## Voice volume

Customers at Eagleson said the voice prompts were too quiet (2026-10-22). The media stream the
voice plays on sat at 10/15 on 255 (253's level was not visible from Cael); the speaker is small
and the bay is loud.

- `VoiceVolume.ensureFull`: raises the voice stream (STREAM_MUSIC) to its max when the app starts
  and before every prompt, without showing the volume panel. Someone turning it down on site is
  undone at the next prompt.
- Loudness enhancer +2 dB -> +4 dB (`TtsManager.LOUDNESS_GAIN_MB`). +6 dB clipped on the Q3mini
  speaker on 2026-10-07; +4 dB is to be confirmed by ear on 255 before installing on 253.
- Heartbeats carry `voice_volume` / `voice_volume_max`, copied to `devices`
  (`docs/migrations/2026-10-22_voice_volume.sql`). Until it is applied the terminal resends the
  heartbeat without them (PostgREST rejects unknown columns).
- Cael (gs-ssp-cmp): device panel shows "Voice volume 15 / 15"; device list badge when it is
  below full or muted.

Includes everything in 1.1.7 / 1.2.6 (scanner retry and diagnostics, remote scanner test,
Supabase session fix). Install on 253 / 255 (Wash) and 254 (Timer); check HOME in the system
check after installing.
