# Aegis Wash 1.1.10 -- next release after 1.1.9

Changes: Simulation Mode is gone, and the payment page acts on the first choice only.

## Simulation Mode removed (owner, 2026-10-31) -- urgent
On 1.1.9 a long press of the version label (bottom-left corner of Home) switched Simulation Mode
on, with no PIN. In that mode a card sale waited 3 s and "paid" without the reader, and the wash
was "started" by a mock adapter: no charge, no wash. On 253 it went on at 2026-10-30 15:37 with
nobody meaning to (an outdoor bay -- a hand or water on the screen) and stayed on until staff
rebooted at 2026-10-31 13:03 because "card tap did nothing": 43 sales recorded as PAID,
entry_mode SIMULATED ($252; also 2 on 10-27). Those 45 rows ($268) were moved to
`backup.transactions_253_simulated_20261031` and removed from transactions.

Now there is no Simulation Mode at all: no long press, no switch in the technician tools
("HARDWARE SIMULATION MODE" row gone), no fake card sale, no mock dispense. Health light,
diagnostics, test pulses, watchdog and SN always use the real hardware. Three taps on the
version label still open the PIN-protected technician tools.

## Payment page acts on the first choice only (2026-10-30, commit 1544a4a)
The card countdown and a tap on Card at the same moment started two sales 59 ms apart; PAYWizard
refused one as busy (253, 2026-10-29 20:26, CARD_READER_FAULT, no charge). Only the first of
countdown / Card / Scan / Cancel now counts.

## Install
253 via TMS (`aegis-wash-1.1.10-signed.apk`). Until it is installed, 253 can still fall into
Simulation Mode from a long press -- check transactions for entry_mode SIMULATED.
