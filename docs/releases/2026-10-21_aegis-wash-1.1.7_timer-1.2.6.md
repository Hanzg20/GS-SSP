# Aegis Wash 1.1.7 / Aegis Timer 1.2.6 -- next release after 1.1.6 / 1.2.5

Scanner faults (253 refused 8 scans in ten minutes on 2026-10-21, also on 2026-10-07: the
scan service answered "not open" within 1-37 ms, before trying the camera).

- `WizarPosScannerProvider`: a refused open is closed, waited 1 s and retried once with a fresh
  device handle.
- When both tries fail, `SCANNER_FAULT` says why: scan service version / enabled, camera
  permission, whether another app holds the camera, minutes since the last good read.
- System check: "Scanner (coupon / VIP)" row with the same line and a Test button (opens the
  scanner, closes after 15 s, says what came back).

Checked on 255: the row shows "scan service 4.0.38; camera permission ok; cam0=free"; Test opened
the camera and closed after 15 s. The retry itself only shows when the service refuses, which
did not happen on 255.

Install on 253 / 255 (Wash) and 254 (Timer); check HOME in the system check after installing.

## Remote scanner test (SCANNER_TEST)
- Cael device panel: "Scanner test". The terminal, when nobody is buying or washing (the ad
  screen counts as idle), opens its scanner for up to 4 s and closes it; the command row gets
  SUCCESS / FAILED and `device_commands.result` (`docs/migrations/2026-10-21_device_command_result.sql`,
  applied), e.g. "Scanner opened. scan service 4.0.38; camera permission ok; cam0=IN USE".
  Late commands (> 1 min) expire, like REBOOT. Verified on 255.
- An app that doesn't know a command leaves its status alone (two GoldSky apps on one
  terminal both receive it).

## Session kept across other apps' screens
`install(Auth) { enableLifecycleCallbacks = false }`: with the callbacks on, Auth reloaded the
session when our app came back to the front, and a request in that moment went out with only
the publishable key -- RLS matched nothing, silently. Seen on 255: a card sale's PENDING insert
as PAYWizard's screen came up (queued offline, written 13 s later) and the scanner test's final
status right after the scanner page closed (lost). Affects every app on core/data.

## Installing Timer on a Wash terminal takes HOME
Seen again on 255: after installing Timer 1.2.6 the HOME holder was Timer. Check the system
check's Default home after any install.

## Added 2026-10-22: scanner would not open after the kiosk screen was recreated (the real cause)
`WizarPosScannerProvider` kept the Activity it was created from, and the cloudpos SDK binds the
scan service with that context on every `open()`. Once that Activity was destroyed (OPC covering
the kiosk, a HOME relaunch -- the provider outlives it), the system refused the binding
("W/ActivityManager: Binding with unknown activity") and `open()` returned false in 1-30 ms:
253 on 2026-10-07 / 10-21, 255 on 10-22 from 22:58 (task 3089 -> 3090) on. Scans failed until
the app process restarted. Now it uses the application context. 1.1.7's retry/diagnostics stay
(the diagnostics showed "camera permission ok; cam0=free" -- the camera itself was fine).

## Added 2026-10-22: voice volume

Customers at Eagleson said the voice prompts were too quiet (2026-10-22). The media stream the
voice plays on sat at 10/15 on 255 (253's level was not visible from Cael); the speaker is small
and the bay is loud.

- `VoiceVolume.ensureFull`: raises the voice stream (STREAM_MUSIC) to its max when the app starts
  and before every prompt, without showing the volume panel. Someone turning it down on site is
  undone at the next prompt.
- Loudness enhancer +2 dB -> +4 dB (`TtsManager.LOUDNESS_GAIN_MB`). +6 dB clipped on the Q3mini
  speaker on 2026-10-07; +4 dB confirmed by ear on 255 (2026-10-22).
- Heartbeats carry `voice_volume` / `voice_volume_max`, copied to `devices`
  (`docs/migrations/2026-10-22_voice_volume.sql`). Until it is applied the terminal resends the
  heartbeat without them (PostgREST rejects unknown columns).
- Cael (gs-ssp-cmp): device panel shows "Voice volume 15 / 15"; device list badge when it is
  below full or muted.

Added to 1.1.7 / 1.2.6 on 2026-10-22, before release (the owner kept the version number).
Checked on 255: the camera opens after the kiosk screen was recreated; the voice is loud enough
and clean at 15/15 + 4 dB.
