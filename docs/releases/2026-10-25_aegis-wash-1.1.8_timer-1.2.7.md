# Aegis Wash 1.1.8 / Aegis Timer 1.2.7 -- next release after 1.1.7 / 1.2.6

Wash stays 1.1.8 (owner, 2026-10-25), the number the 255 test builds already used. A terminal
holding one of those test builds has the same versionCode: install it with `adb install -r`
(TMS may skip it as already installed).

## Offline start-ups no longer leave a stray anonymous user (core/data)
A saved session whose refresh couldn't reach Supabase (Wi-Fi not up yet after a power cut) reads
as `SessionStatus.NetworkError`, and `currentSessionOrNull()` is null then -- but the SDK still
holds it and retries every 10 s. `ensureAuthenticated()` took that for "never signed in" and
signed in anonymously; the SDK's retry put the old session back seconds later. One throwaway
user + `device_auth_map` row per offline start-up: 254 three in four days, 255 had 292, SN_UNKNOWN
39 (cleaned up 2026-10-25, backups in `backup.*_20261025`). Now it waits up to 30 s for that
retry and never signs in while the saved session is only unreachable.

Checked on 255: apps stopped, Wi-Fi off 62 min (sessions expired), started offline, Wi-Fi back:
"Saved session's refresh is retrying; waiting for it instead of signing in", then the same user
(d7237af7) refreshed; no new user, still 3 links.

## Remote "Bring to front" (BRING_TO_FRONT)
253 sat on OPC's page after its reboot on 2026-10-25 and nobody on site could leave it.
- Cael device panel (gs-ssp-cmp): "Bring to front". The HOME app, when nobody is paying (wash /
  timer session counts as busy), starts its kiosk screen / HOME entry up to 4 times, 3 s apart,
  and writes the outcome to `device_commands.result`, e.g. "Back in front (#1 kiosk: in front)".
  Late commands (> 1 min) expire, like REBOOT. On a two-app terminal only the HOME holder takes it.
- `KioskRecall` (core/ui), sharing `BootFrontGuard.relaunch`.

Checked on 255 (Wash): OPC opened over the kiosk 5 min after boot; the command came back SUCCESS
on the first try. Timer's handler is the same code, not run on a device yet.

Not solved: on 253 / 254 OPC comes to the front by itself after every boot and the boot guard's
6 tries all stay covered (254: 10-24 21:53, 10-25 12:20 / 12:23). On 255 OPC doesn't do that and
the guard gets the kiosk back at once, so 255 can't reproduce it. Question to WizarPOS:
`docs/wizarpos_opc_front_question_2026-10-25.md`. The command may fail the same way there.

## VIP page without plans
The page used to keep an old guide ("ask our attendant...") when the plans couldn't be loaded,
offering options that couldn't work. Now:
- couldn't reach the server: "Network disconnected / VIP top-up is unavailable. Please try again
  later." (spoken) -- a card can't be charged offline either, so no "pay by card";
- the store has no plans: "VIP top-up unavailable / Please pay by card for now."
- "Choose how much to load" is only spoken once the plans are on screen.
The old guide and its strings are gone. Shared VipActivity: Wash, Laundry, Iris, Ourea.

Checked on 255: online the 2 x 2 plans show (owner); Wi-Fi off, the page shows and speaks the
network message.

## Timer: fewer small lines, bigger key text (owner, 2026-10-25)
Same rule as Wash's 2026-10-21 pass: small explanation lines out, what the customer acts on bigger.
- Removed: "Choose your time", "Card · Tap", "Sorry for the inconvenience", "IN USE" under a
  unit's countdown, the package name and the two card-reader hints on the paying screen
  (PAYWizard's screen covers ours during a sale), "remaining", "Stops automatically when time
  runs out", "The countdown is on the main screen.", "from your VIP balance", "by card after you
  confirm", the coupon expiry date.
- Bigger: "Choose the number shown on your vacuum" 18sp, "Available" 17sp, minutes on the price
  buttons 19sp, START 20sp, "Coupon / VIP code" 17sp, notice bar 17sp, out of service 26sp,
  "Almost done -- finish up!" 24sp (only in the last 30 s), result text 20sp, confirm lines 18sp.

- Second pass, explanations with no use: "Thank you -- see you next time!" after time's up (the
  title stays), the first line of "Card payment unavailable" (repeated the title), "Please see the
  attendant" after a code / coupon / VIP card that can't be used (unattended site). Insufficient
  VIP balance no longer says "Top up on the VIP page" -- Timer has no VIP page -- just the balance
  and "Please pay by card." Money lines stay (no charge made, reversal / refund steps).
- Third pass: "Please try again or use another card." after a decline (the card reader says it),
  "or top up" when the VIP balance is short; "Your payment has been reversed. No charge was made."
  is one line: "Payment reversed. No charge was made."

Checked on 255 (dual home, test store now also has Eagleson's two vacuum packages): layout fits.
The paying / running / result / confirm screens need a sale or a scan and were not opened.
Installing Timer took HOME from Wash again on 255; given back with `cmd role add-role-holder`.

## Command poll window 10 min -> 12 h
Commands Realtime missed while the terminal was offline are now picked up (and START_SERVICE /
REBOOT / SCANNER_TEST / BRING_TO_FRONT older than 1 min expired) instead of staying PENDING.
