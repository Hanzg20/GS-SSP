# Aegis Wash 1.1.6 / Aegis Timer 1.2.5 — 2026-10-21

Fix for 253 staying on the OPC page after a reboot (2026-10-21, 16:07 and 18:05 reboots), which
1.1.5 did not fix. Signed with the WizarPOS-certified release key.

## Cause
`BootFrontGuard` decided "covered" by counting our activities' start/stop callbacks. The splash
starts before the guard is installed, so the count was off: on 255 (a covered Wash, made with
`monkey -p com.wizarpos.opc`) it never acted at all, and on 253 it relaunched once, judged that
relaunch "still covered", and stopped. It also acted only at the moment of being covered, so OPC
coming to the front again after its start-up was never handled.

Android did not block anything: the HOME app's background start is allowed
(`BAL_ALLOW_ALLOWLISTED_COMPONENT` in 255's log).

## Change
- Every 3 s during the first 5 min after boot the guard asks the system whether the app is in
  the foreground (`ActivityManager.getMyMemoryState`) — no counting.
- Covered → relaunch, alternating the kiosk activity and the app's HOME entry, up to 6 times;
  same conditions as before (untouched screen, HOME holder).
- One `KIOSK_COVERED_AT_BOOT` report per boot with the timeline, sent once the kiosk has stayed
  in front for 30 s or the guard gives up, e.g.
  `back in front; 84s covered, #1 kiosk, 87s in front, 96s covered, #2 home, 99s in front, ...`

## Verified
255: rebooted, OPC brought over Wash three times (84 s, 96 s, 105 s after boot); each time Wash was
back within 3 s, both relaunch ways worked, report 526 arrived.

## After installing on 253 / 254
Check HOME (tech panel → System check) — installing Wash can take HOME from Timer. After the next
reboot read `KIOSK_COVERED_AT_BOOT` for the timeline.
